### Earthquake (Japan/Taiwan/S.Korea/Philippines/Indonesia/China)  _(source: slide 14)_

**Sub-Types / context:**
- EARTHQUAKES - JAPAN, TAIWAN, SOUTH KOREA, PHILLIPPINES, INDONESIA, AND CHINA

**Sub-Types:**
- Naturally caused earthquakes
- Shaking caused by bombs and tests
- Shaking caused by Volcanic activities
- Tsunamis

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- When magnitude is above or equal to 6.4, send Pre-Alert within 25 minutes once the feed is received on the Resilinc Feed Capturing System.
- When magnitude is below 6.4, send an alert within 45 minutes.
- We do not notify all earthquakes. Alerting happens based on the following thresholds provided by Resilinc:
- For Japan, Taiwan, South Korea, Philippines, Indonesia, & China:
- Notify Magnitude 5.0 or above straightaway when:- Epicenter on land and sites mapped in the region- Epicenter on land, no sites mapped but an important region- Epicenter off the coast and sites mapped in the coastal regions- Epicenter off the coast/Tsunami Alert issued
- Check GDACS and USGS for details on earthquakes.
- NOTE:
- If we have supplier sites in the region, issue a customer impact with Pre-Alert and request supplier impact confirmation
- If/when disruptions reported, send an update within 2 hours of the Pre-Alert
- If further damages are reported, send an update every 6 hours (based on the availability of fresh information)
- For Japan/Taiwan region, provide a list of regions with a Shindo scale- JMA Website
- If no sites are mapped, but an important manufacturing country, send a LOW FYI News and save an empty polygon
- If no sites are mapped, but important infrastructure in the region including, but not limited to, ports, airports, power plants, dams, major border crossings, etc., send a LOW FYI News and save an empty polygon

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Always create a WarRoom with Pre-Alert
- For Japan and Taiwan, create polygon based on Shindo scale covering regions 5 lower (-5) and above, if available within 25 minutes)
- If Shindo scale is not available, draw a radius around epicenter
- Polygon Radius:
- Note: Update the impacted region if further damages are reported in the extended region, outside the initial polygon.

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (send with pre-alert)

### Environmental Hazard  _(source: slide 16)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Site/Factory pollutant emissions
- Air quality concerns after Spills/leaks
- Protests/actions on emissions
- Regional-level Smog/health alerts
- Neglect and Contamination, New/Update Regulations
- River pollution/effects on water treatment
- Fines/Bans/Investigations
- Dust Storms
- Governments actions and their impact

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- Smog Alert-Notify highest level (Red) smog/pollution alerts if sites mapped in the region of impact (send impact)
- Partner site(s) involved in air, water, or river pollution, high emissions rate observed at the site, etc.? Notify straightaway (send customer impact)
- If the company is not mapped - Send a bulletin if products have applications in the industries, we cover. Possible sub-tier or Tier 1 yet to be mapped (decide based on product line)
- Service provider involved? Notify if applications in our verticals
- Note: Send updates on reported or potential citations, lawsuits, investigations, etc. If companies confirm operational disruptions, move to HIGH and send supplier impact

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Partner site(s) involved? Send impact to the exact site
- Regional event? Send impact if the site(s) mapped in the region of impact
- Radius:
- Low = Impact not needed
- Medium = Affected region
- High = Affected + 10 km
- Severe = Affected + 30 km
- Send supplier impact only when High or Severe
- If there are companies confirming disruptions to operations, move to HIGH and send supplier impact

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** Yes (when disruptions have been confirmed by companies)

### Extreme Weather  _(source: slide 17)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Nor'easters, Bomb cyclone, Arctic Outbreaks/blast, Ice Storms, Polar Vortex
- Major Winter/Ice Storms
- Lake-effect snow warnings

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- Report only highly disruptive weather scenarios (follow Hurricane/Typhoon criteria for major named hurricanes, cyclones, and typhoons).
- DO NOT REPORT thunderstorms and minor frequent weather disruptions.
- Notify extreme weather situations only if partner sites are in the region.
- Notify only if infrastructure damage, transportation disruptions, flight cancellations, or utility service disruptions are reported.
- Power outages: Notify if partner sites are found in the region.
- Monitor delivery and logistics services for transportation disruptions and report them if observed.
- Major Named Snowstorms: Notify proactively as soon as they are identified before the storm reaches the region.
- For smaller storms, if disruptions are reported within areas outside the initial polygon, create a new event instead of an update.

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Regions where supplier sites are present, create a WarRoom via geofencing
- Extreme weather polygon should cover most affected regions (Red zones only)
- Check if we have reports of a predicted path of the storm
- Check which regions are mentioned in the reports of disruptions
- If a storm system is more than 3 days old and is impacting a newer region, it is preferred that we do not update it with new suppliers and sites. Instead, a new WarRoom should be created.

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** We do not issue a "Supplier Impact Confirmation Request" for every extreme weather situation, as the supplier community does not appreciate it. The "Supplier Impact Confirmation Request" is issued only when: There are companies in the affected region publicly confirming disruptions to operations. There are signs of evacuations, power outages, impacts on utility services, etc. There are significantly affected areas (red zones) with multiple reports of disruptions. Note: There are some cases where there is a need to issue an impact confirmation request for a previously published update. This happens when reports of disruptions start surfacing the day after the event was published.

### Flood  _(source: slide 23)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Site flooding |Region flooding | Flash flooding | Flash flood warning | Dam collapse risk | Flood Warning | Landslides | Major Road closures | industrial park flooding | Major border crossing affected |  Post-hurricane/storm flooding | Localized flooding | Widespread power outages | Evacuations | Airport/Port inundated

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- We notify our customers about flooding and flash flooding events because structural damages and transportation restrictions can cause supply chain disruptions.
- Flooding events should be notified as soon reported in public domain. We should be notifying flooding events pro-actively if there are warnings and evacuations in the region.
- We notify flooding events if we have supplier sites(s) in the region of impact
- Other than supplier sites, we also check if any services or any of the following operations are affected:
- Logistics providers, cargo shipping, utility provider, farming, ports, airports, postal services, train & freight, mining, data centers, power grids, nuclear power plants and more
- NOTE:
- If a dam is expected to burst or a river is expected to break its banks, we should analyze which regions could get flooded due to the excessive water
- We should geofence those regions proactively and create WarRooms
- If disruptions and damages reported, send an update every 6 to 8 hours
- We can report Flood Warning but DO NOT report Flood Watch or Flood Advisory

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Regions where supplier sites are present, create a WarRoom via geofencing the region. The size of the polygon depends on:
- Radius:•  Check if we have reports of a predicted path of the flood
- •  Check which regions are mentioned in the reports of disruptions
- •  Flooding event polygon should cover the most affected regions (in major floods cover neighboring regions as well)
- Note: Update the impacted region if further damages are reported in the extended region, outside the initial polygon.

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only)

### Forest Fire  _(source: slide 25)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Wildfires near supplier sites
- Wildfires heading toward the important manufacturing region
- Wildfire causing major road closures
- Wildfires causing air traffic disruptions
- Wildfires affecting utility services
- Wildfires leading to evacuations
- Wildfires affecting mining regions

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- Notify only if partner site(s) in the region of impact
- Is the fire still growing? Also, check expanded regions that    are on fire watch or warnings (figure out which direction the fire is expanding)
- Check if any industrial parks, mines, major roads, port(s) and airport(s), etc. are potentially affected and notify
- Check if evacuations, power outages, road closures, or flight cancelations are announced because of the wildfire.
- Check if a service or utility provider(s) potentially affected
- Notify if potential freight/cargo disruptions expected
- Send updates if the situation worsens

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Send impact when:
- Evacuations reported/ high alerts issued for the region
- Partner site(s) in the region of evacuations/warnings
- Radius:
- Low = No impact needed
- Medium = Affected region
- High = Affected + 10 km
- Severe = Affected + 30 km
- Follow the radius given in the public domain

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only)

**Sub-Types:**
- Outbreaks
- Pandemics / Epidemics
- Health emergencies/crisis
- Lockdowns/curfews
- Travel advisories/restrictions
- Leadership health issues
- Regional restrictions/no-entry zones

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- Major outbreak, pandemic, or epidemic declared
- Partner site(s) in the region of impact
- The important manufacturing region, travel restrictions, labor disruptions, etc.
- (Bird flu, Ebola, MERS, Zika, dengue fever, cholera, SARS, plague, malaria, hepatitis B, etc.)
- Notify if a leader of a supplier company is severely sick
- List of epidemics
- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_epidemics
- COVID UPDATE? Once verified for supply chain disruptions, Covid updates should be alerted within 6 hours.
- Restrictions, Lockdowns, shutdowns, warnings, red zones, spikes, etc. reported and verified on credible sources, and notify within 6 hours.

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Send impact when:
- Partner site(s) in the region of impact
- Supplier company declares spike in infections  Send impact to the sites in the affected city/state/country only
- A country, state, or city declares a spike in infections  Send impact to all sites involved in the regions.

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only)

### Hurricane/Typhoon (Pre-Landfall)  _(source: slide 29)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Named storms | Category storms | Unnamed major storms with high potential | No category storms with high potential | Developing storms | Developed storms | Hurricane/ Typhoon/Cyclone | Tropical storms with potential to grow
- (PRE-LANDFALL)

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- INITIAL BULLETIN:
- When the storm starts shaping and landfall is certain
- Possibly a named major storm
- Threatening important manufacturing region
- Sites/Ports/airports in its path
- UPDATE:
- Forecasted path is known now on GDACS
- If sites are mapped in its path, send customer impact (do not send supplier impact at this point)
- NEXT UPDATE:
- If/when the storm changes its category or path, send an update. (do not send supplier impact until landfall)
- Note:EventWatch sends updates when
- A hurricane changes its category/path
- Emergency or Evacuation warnings issued
- Businesses start shutting down
- New damages and disruption reports emerge
- Warnings are lifted
- Storm starts degrading

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Category 1 or above:
- Send customer impact when the path becomes known
- Send supplier impact when it makes landfall
- Follow the path provided by forecasters (GDACS)
- Tropical storm/No Name/No category
- Send impact on landfall
- Send supplier impact
- NOTE:
- If a storm changes its path pre-landfall, create a new polygon based on a new path. Include the entire path in the new polygon
- If the hurricane is projected to impact a new state within the same country, continue updating the existing WarRoom.
- If the hurricane is projected to impact a new country, or more than one countries, create a separate WarRoom for each country.
- Country name should be mentioned in the title
- If a region already impacted by a hurricane is likely to be hit again by a new hurricane, create a separate WarRoom for the new hurricane.

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only) YES, as soon as authorities start shutting down businesses in preparation for an approaching storm (a storm is several days away from making landfall)

### Power Outage  _(source: slide 37)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Power Outages due to extreme weather
- Blackout at manufacturing facility/industrial park/transportation hub
- Internet outages
- Planned
- Site level
- Regional level =>6 hours for ROW

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- We notify power outages only in a region with sites mapped.
- We notify if Expected Recovery Time is not provided
- For regions with semiconductor fabs, including Taiwan, South Korea, China, Japan, USA, Singapore, Germany, Israel, Netherlands, Malaysia etc. – report all power outages even if the expected recovery time is less than 6 hours
- Rest of the World - If the outage lasts or is expected to last longer than 6 hours
- Power out at a site, warehouse, port, airport, mine, industrial park, etc.
- Important manufacturing regions affected
- NOTE: It is recommended that we check Power Service Provider’s Twitter and website for the most recent updates and outage maps

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Send impact if partner site(s) in the region
- Polygon Radius: Affected region (follow outage map on provider's website)
- Note: If an Explosion/Fire at a power grid, send impact to the region served by the grid

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES Global Rule: Do not send Supplier Impact Confirmation for LOW severity events. For regions known for Semi Conductor fabs, issue Supplier Impact ASAP If there is a Man-made (due to glitch) power outage at supplier location, request impact confirmation ASAP Planned power outages, request impact confirmation only if the outage lasts/expected to last longer than 24 hours For Power Outage caused by extreme weather/storms, issue impact if the outage lasts/expected to last longer than 6 hours

### Tornado  _(source: slide 44)_

**Sub-Types:**
- Tornadoes touchdown
- Power Outages
- Site level impacts
- Vicinity level impacts
- Cargo/Utility impacts
- Major roads in the region
- Infrastructure Impact

**Reporting Guidelines (verbatim from source):**
- Warnings issued? Check if the site(s) are mapped in the region and keep an eye (Monitor)
- Tornado hits, cause structural damages? Notify if the site(s) mapped in the exact vicinity/village/community
- Notify if road closures, business closures, power outages, airport/port disruptions, or utility disruptions reported
- Note: Unlike major storms, tornadoes affect smaller vicinities. We cannot flag an entire state or city for a Tornado event e.g., if a tornado hits Milpitas, California, we should find the exact region in Milpitas where it caused damages

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- Partner site(s) in the region of impact

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only)

- Poor visibility
- Tsunami due to volcano
- Wildfires due to volcano
- Power outages amid eruption

- Notify even if no sites in the region
- Possible aviation disruptions
- Notify as soon as Signs of Eruptions reported
- Reports of volcanic activity/evacuations/high-alert
- Check if Partner site(s) mapped in the region
- Check if important manufacturing hubs/mining/ports/ airports are present in the region.
- Transportation and power disruptions expected
- NOTE: Send frequent updates when additional information becomes available

**WarRoom Guidelines (context; not the impact call itself):**
- When warnings are issued/evacuations ordered
- Partner site(s) in the region of impact
- Radius:
- Low = No impact needed
- Medium = Affected region
- High = Affected + 50 km
- Severe = Affected + 200 km

**Send Supplier Impact Confirmation?** YES (not for LOW severity; MEDIUM, HIGH, SEVERE only)
