## Instructions for Analysts (verbatim)

- P0 - Most Critical Events:
- Stop working on your current task and immediately prioritize P0 events.
- Notify the stakeholders as soon as possible without any delay.
- P1 - Critical Events:
- Consider these events critical and notify them within 3 hours.
- Ensure thorough research but prioritize speed and accuracy.
- P2 - High Priority Events:
- Notify these events within 3 hours.
- These may not be as critical as P1 but require prompt action to avoid any delays.
- P3 - Regular Events:
- Handle these events as part of your regular workflow.
- Notifications should be prepared without urgency but with standard accuracy and thoroughness.
- P4 - Low Priority Events:
- Address these events as a low-priority task.
- Notifications can be prepared after more critical events have been handled.
- Escalation Rule:
- If multiple events occur simultaneously, always prioritize in the order of P0 > P1 > P2 > P3 > P4.
- Keep the team lead informed of high-volume scenarios for additional resource allocation.
- This structure ensures that critical events receive immediate attention, while less critical events are addressed systematically.
